DeepGuard Multi-Modal Architecture (7 AI Engine Modules)

DeepGuard orchestrates 7 defense-grade artificial intelligence and heuristic modules to detect fake news, manipulated media, and viral misinformation campaigns.

1. Text Analysis (DistilBERT)

Fine-tuned DistilBERT Sequence Classifier trained on benchmark misinformation datasets.

2. Image Detection (EfficientNetV2-S)

Fine-tuned EfficientNetV2-S Deep Neural Network with dual-engine ONNX runtime for detecting synthetic and edited news images.

3. Metadata Analysis (EXIF)

EXIF metadata parser extracting camera make, software modification flags, timestamp & GPS coordinates.

4. Sentiment Intensity (VADER)

VADER lexicon-based sentiment analysis for detecting emotional exaggeration and outrage baiting.

5. Viral Threat Pattern

Custom regex heuristic rules targeting chain messages, WhatsApp forward threats & false urgency.

6. Context Mismatch (CLIP)

OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/32 vision-language model evaluating semantic correlation between text and image.

7. Explainable AI (SHAP)

SHAP feature attribution generator providing word importance and plain-language explanations.
