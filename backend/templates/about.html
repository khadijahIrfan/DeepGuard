ABOUT DEEPGUARD

Multimodal Detection of Manipulated Digital Content Using Text and Image Analysis

DeepGuard is an AI-powered system developed as a Final Year Project to detect manipulated digital content across multiple modalities. Unlike traditional systems that analyze only text or only images, DeepGuard combines Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Metadata Analysis, Sentiment Analysis, Virality Detection, and Context Matching to provide more reliable content verification.
