ABOUT DEEPGUARD

Multimodal Fake Content Detection System

DeepGuard is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform developed as a Final Year Project. Unlike traditional fake news detectors that analyze only text or only images, DeepGuard performs multimodal analysis by combining Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, metadata inspection, sentiment analysis, virality detection, and context matching to produce a more reliable prediction.
